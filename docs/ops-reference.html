DOCUMENT REGISTRY

NINE DOCS.
ONE OPERATION.

Every SPCTR document has one job. This guide tells you what that job is, who it belongs to, and exactly when to deploy it. Read this once — then you won't have to guess.

01
Client-Facing Sales
Service Overview

Your pitch deck in document form. Eight pages covering who SPCTR is, the two engagement models, the delivery process, the fit profile, and how to get started. Polished, branded, and built to convert a skeptical prospect into a booked call. This is the first document a prospect ever sees.

Used By
Esteban (Founder)
Used When
Send to a warm lead before or immediately after a first conversation. Attach to outreach follow-ups. Drop in a LinkedIn DM when a prospect asks "what do you do?"
Trigger
→ Any qualified prospect who asks about SPCTR
02
Internal
Ops Brief

SPCTR's internal business operations and delivery plan. Covers the full model — mission, positioning, both offerings, the delivery workflow, tools, and expansion path. Not a client document. This is the north star that keeps every campaign decision consistent with how the business is supposed to run.

Used By
Esteban (Founder)
Used When
Reference when making positioning or pricing decisions. Re-read when onboarding a new type of client or expanding into a new niche. Keep it current as the model evolves.
Trigger
→ Model decisions, pricing changes, expansion planning
03
Client-Facing Collaborative
Client Intake & ICP Brief

The campaign's foundation document. Filled out collaboratively with the client — covers their company profile, engagement scope, ICP (titles, firmographics, geographies, disqualifiers), the value proposition SPCTR will lead with, and the meeting qualification bar. Nothing in the campaign builds until both parties have signed this. No signed brief = no work starts.

Used By
Esteban + Client (collaborative)
Used When
Completed on or immediately after the kickoff call. Must be signed before Apollo prospecting, domain setup, or any sequence work begins. Referenced throughout the campaign to keep targeting and messaging on-brief.
Trigger
→ Client signs the MSA + first payment received
04
Internal Ops
Campaign Launch Checklist

The operational runbook for every SPCTR campaign — from signed intake brief to first send. Broken into phase-gated sections: pre-flight, Apollo prospecting, sending infrastructure, Instantly setup, warmup, list load, sequence build, and launch. Every phase has a gate that must be cleared before moving forward. This doc prevents the deliverability and quality failures that kill cold campaigns.

Used By
Esteban (Strategist)
Used When
Open this the moment the Intake Brief is signed and first payment is received. Work it top-to-bottom for every new campaign. Never skip a gate. One checklist per campaign — keep a copy in the client's Drive folder.
Trigger
→ Intake Brief signed + payment confirmed
05
Internal Triage
Meeting Qualification Rubric

The reply triage guide. Every inbound reply gets scored across six dimensions: Authority, Vendor Fit, Partner Tier & Profile, Pain or Pipeline Signal, Reply Tone, and Timing Intent. A reply must hit HOT on at least four of six (including Authority and Vendor Fit) to be classified HOT and booked. WARM replies enter a nurture branch. PASS replies get suppressed. This is the standard that keeps SPCTR from delivering junk meetings.

Used By
Esteban (Strategist)
Used When
Applied to every reply, every campaign, every client. Open it when a reply comes in and you're deciding whether to book. When in doubt, score all six dimensions before deciding — don't go by gut alone.
Trigger
→ Any prospect reply during an active campaign
06
Shared with Client Billing Source
Meeting Delivery Template

A live Google Sheet shared with each client at campaign launch. One row per accepted meeting. SPCTR fills all prospect data within 24 hours of booking. Client fills the Outcome column within 48 hours of the meeting. Three tabs: Meetings (main log), Suppression (do-not-contact list), and Summary (read-only metrics). This sheet is the source of truth for billing — per-meeting invoices are reconciled directly against it.

Used By
SPCTR (fills) + Client (statuses)
Used When
Created at campaign launch and shared with the client. Updated by SPCTR every time a meeting is booked. Client updates outcomes after each meeting. Reference during billing disputes — the sheet row is the invoice line item.
Trigger
→ Campaign launches / every meeting booked
07
Legal Client-Facing
Client Service Agreement

The master agreement — the legal foundation of every SPCTR client relationship. Covers scope of service, deliverables and timelines, fees and payment (Net 7, ACH/card), client responsibilities, confidentiality, IP ownership, limitation of liability, and termination (30 days' written notice). Month-to-month by default. Signed once per client. All SOWs attach under this document — if a SOW conflicts with the MSA, the SOW controls for that engagement only.

Used By
Both parties (Esteban + Client)
Used When
Sent to the client before any SOW is signed. Must be executed before any campaign work begins. Signed once — covers the full relationship. Every future SOW or engagement attaches under it without re-signing the MSA.
Trigger
→ Client says yes — before any SOW or work begins
08
Legal Client-Facing
Statement of Work — Pay Per Meeting

The engagement contract for PPM clients. Attaches to the MSA. Locks in the ICP, scope, and performance-based pricing: $2,500 one-time setup fee (non-refundable) + $1,500 per accepted meeting. 90-day minimum engagement window so SPCTR can recoup infrastructure investment. No monthly volume guarantee — client pays only for what gets booked. After 90 days, converts to month-to-month.

Used By
Both parties (Esteban + Client)
Used When
Sent alongside or immediately after the MSA for any Pay Per Meeting engagement. Requires MSA to already be signed. Use this for first-time clients who want to test SPCTR before committing to a retainer.
Trigger
→ Client chooses the PPM model + MSA is signed
09
Legal Client-Facing
Statement of Work — Monthly Retainer

The engagement contract for Retainer clients. Attaches to the MSA. Covers dedicated outbound capacity at $7,500/month — setup included in month one. Target band: 8–12 accepted meetings/month at full ramp. Below 8: $500/meeting credit (capped $2,000). Above 12: $1,000/meeting overage. 3-month initial term, then month-to-month. One pause per quarter (up to 30 days, no charge). Ramp period is the first 45 days — credits don't apply during ramp.

Used By
Both parties (Esteban + Client)
Used When
Sent alongside or after the MSA for any Monthly Retainer engagement. Use for clients who are committed to consistent pipeline and want the full outbound function managed end-to-end. Often the upgrade path after a successful PPM campaign.
Trigger
→ Client chooses the Retainer model + MSA is signed
