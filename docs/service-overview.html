FIT_PROFILE

WHO SPCTR
IS BUILT FOR.

We qualify our clients as carefully as we qualify your prospects.

GREEN LIGHT — GREAT FIT
B2B company with a defined ICP and a sales team ready to work meetings
Average deal size $3K+ — cold email ROI needs room to breathe
Someone on your team who can follow up within hours, not days
Realistic timeline expectations — outbound is a 60–90 day game
Open to sharing what's worked (and what hasn't) in past sales cycles
YELLOW LIGHT — CONSIDER CAREFULLY
B2C or very low-ticket — economics get thin below $1K deal size
No defined ICP — we can help scope it, but it adds timeline
Sales cycle longer than 12 months — attribution gets complex
Highly regulated industries — compliance review needed before launch
Not sure if you're a fit? Just book the call. The intake conversation is designed to figure that out together — no pressure either way.
